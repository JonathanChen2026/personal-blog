The sun was setting fast, and after sprinting up the stairs of the Yellow Crane Tower, I was met with one of the most extraordinary sunsets I have ever seen. With only a few minutes to spare, I sent the drone up and tried exposure bracketing for the first time, manually shooting three exposures to stack later. It helped overcome a lot of my old drone’s limitations, since it couldn’t shoot RAW or bracket automatically. With security guards nearby and the light disappearing by the second, the whole moment felt incredibly thrilling.
